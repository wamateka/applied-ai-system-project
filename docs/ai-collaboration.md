# AI Collaboration Notes

## How AI helped during the project

AI was used as a development partner to explore architecture options, pressure-test the workflow, and refine how the new feature should fit the existing pet-care app instead of replacing it.

## Helpful suggestion

One especially helpful suggestion was to add a self-check stage after planning. That changed the design in a useful way: every recommendation now has to survive citation matching, duplicate-task checks, and safety validation before it reaches the user.

## Flawed or incorrect suggestion

An early suggestion was to expand the feature into health triage and symptom advice. That would have made the system riskier and less responsible, because the app does not have medical-grade data or clinical oversight. The design was corrected by narrowing the AI scope to routine-care planning and adding emergency guardrails.
